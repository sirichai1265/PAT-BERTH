P.B = pilot boarding / berthing time. Times on the sheet are written DD/HH; the month is taken from the sheet date (Sep–Oct 2026). Gate stoppages (G.29, G.25) reduce crane/gate capacity at the berth but do not close it. A historical sheet freezes "Now" to that day's snapshot time.
